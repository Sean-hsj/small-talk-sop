# Illustration system

[← Repository home](../../README.md)

These illustrations add pauses and scene memory without carrying required instructions. The documents remain complete when images are hidden, blocked, or viewed with a screen reader.

## Art direction

- handmade editorial pen-and-ink line work;
- pure white panels with high-contrast black strokes;
- one restrained cobalt-blue spot color;
- ordinary workplace gestures and slightly dry humor;
- wide, quiet compositions with generous margins;
- no text, logos, gradients, beige paper effects, 3D rendering, decorative blobs, or corporate clip-art figures.

The white panel is intentional. It preserves the black line work in both GitHub light and dark themes.

## Prompt set

All three assets used the built-in image-generation path and the `illustration-story` use case.

### Coffee hello

> Wide single-frame comic of two coworkers beside an office coffee machine. One gives a small wave; the other holds a mug and keeps an unobstructed walking path. Handmade black pen-and-ink, pure white, sparse cobalt-blue accents, no text or decorative clutter.

### Manager calibration

> Wide three-beat scene without panel borders: an employee offers a short note, a manager points calmly to more detail, and both record one agreed next step. Equal dignity, ordinary gestures, black line work, white field, minimal cobalt-blue accents, no labels.

### Easy-exit lunch

> Wide canteen scene: coworkers continue a relaxed lunch while another colleague waves goodbye and leaves freely through an open door. No pressure to stay, no alcohol, black line work, white field, restrained cobalt-blue accents, no text.

Every prompt also prohibited beige and pastel palettes, gradients, glossy 3D, stock or corporate-vector styling, exaggerated smiles, photorealism, cinematic lighting, uncanny anatomy, watermarks, and an over-polished generated-image finish.

## Production notes

- Generated for this repository on 2026-08-18, then manually selected, inspected, named, and resized.
- Final PNGs are 1,600 pixels wide for the hero and 1,500 pixels wide for section art.
- Each file must remain below 1.2 MB so the guide stays comfortable to browse.
- Do not place instructions or dialogue only inside a raster image.
- New art must pass anatomy, stereotype, privacy, contrast, alt-text, dark-theme, and file-weight review.
- Add new files rather than silently overwriting an approved asset.
